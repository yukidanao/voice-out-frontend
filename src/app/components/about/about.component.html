About

Curious how this little wall works?

VoiceOut is a small side project with a simple goal: give people a place to be honest without being seen. Here's what it's made of.

Principles

  • Anonymous by default

    No names, no profiles, no tracking of who said what.

  • Kind by design

    Negative posts are removed to keep the wall a safe place.

  • Real-time, always

    New voices appear the moment they're posted. No refresh needed.
